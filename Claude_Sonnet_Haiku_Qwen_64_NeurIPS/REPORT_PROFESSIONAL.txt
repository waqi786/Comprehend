==============================================================================
   KEY FINDINGS
==============================================================================

  1. Sonnet and Haiku perform comparably on both sets, both
     ahead of Qwen 7B on entailment and informativeness.

  2. Haiku shows stronger SPECIFICITY (spec_faith) across
     levels in both sets (peaks at 1.000 on dissimilar L1).

  3. mpnet separates similar vs dissimilar papers far better
     than scincl (+0.485 vs +0.191). scincl gives ~0.687 avg
     similarity for dissimilar papers, i.e. too high to be
     discriminative. ** mpnet retained for pipeline. **

  4. Judge caveat: scores from GPT-4o-mini (not Qwen 3B used
     in Phase 1). Absolute values differ; relative ranking
     across 3 summarizers remains valid.
